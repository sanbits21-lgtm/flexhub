Questions, answered

Do I need a card to start the trial?

No. You register, confirm your email, and we approve your gym. The trial starts when you are approved, and nothing is charged.

Can I move my members from Excel?

Yes. Upload your spreadsheet, match the columns once, and the members and their plans are imported. Duplicate phone numbers are flagged.

Do my members need to install anything?

No store download is needed. Members open a link or scan your QR poster, sign in, and add the app to their home screen.

How do payments reach me?

At the desk, a member pays by UPI QR or cash and you confirm it. The money goes straight to your UPI account. FitzyClub does not hold it.

Can I run more than one branch?

Yes. Add branches, give each a manager, and see them all from one owner view. Each plan lists how many branches it includes.

What happens to my data if I leave?

You can export your member list yourself at any time. For everything else, ask support and we will send you your records.

Is it secure?

Each gym's data is kept separate from every other gym's, passwords are stored hashed, repeated wrong guesses lock the account, and every sign-in session can be ended.
