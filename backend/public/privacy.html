FitzyClub Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{UPDATED}}

FitzyClub is the app and website your gym uses to manage memberships, attendance, payments, classes and training. Your gym ({{OPERATOR}}) decides what is recorded about you and is responsible for it. FitzyClub processes this information only on the gym's behalf, to provide the service.

What is collected

  • Account details entered by your gym: name, phone, email, member code.
  • Profile details you or your gym add: photo, date of birth, gender, height and weight, fitness goal, experience level, diet preference, health notes, emergency contact.
  • Gym activity: check-ins and check-outs, membership plan and dates, payments and receipts, class bookings and personal-training sessions.
  • Workout and diet plans generated for you or written by your trainer.
  • Staff: trainers and managers' name, contact details, role, and the actions they take (an audit log).

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell personal information.

Camera and photos

Staff may use the camera to scan a member's QR pass at the front desk; the image is not stored. Photos you choose are used only as your profile picture. Both permissions can be refused; the rest of the app still works.

AI-generated plans

If your gym has turned on AI suggestions, a workout or diet plan is generated by sending a limited profile to an AI provider (Anthropic): your age, gender, height, weight, goal, level, diet preference, any health notes you wrote, and the gym's equipment names. Your name, phone number and email are never sent. Otherwise plans are made by built-in rules on our own server. Plans are general guidance, not medical advice; talk to a doctor before starting a new programme, especially with a health condition.

Who can see your information

  • Staff of your own gym, according to their role. Staff of other gyms cannot.
  • Our hosting and database providers, who store it for us under their security terms.
  • The AI provider described above, for plan generation only.
  • Authorities, only where the law requires it.

How it is protected and kept

Data is sent over HTTPS, passwords are stored hashed, sign-in sessions are kept in the phone's secure storage, and each gym's data is separated from every other gym's. We keep your information while you are a member and as long as the gym needs its records.

Your choices and deleting your account

  • Delete your account in the app: Profile, then Delete my account. Your name, phone, email, photo, health and body details and saved plans are erased and you are signed out everywhere. Your upcoming sessions and bookings are cancelled.
  • Your gym keeps its own payment and attendance records, which no longer show your name, because it must keep its accounts.
  • To see, correct or export your information, ask your gym, or write to {{CONTACT}}.

Children

Minors should use FitzyClub only with a parent or guardian's consent, given to the gym that registers them.
